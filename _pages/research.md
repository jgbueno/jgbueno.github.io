<td markdown="span">Title
</td>
<td markdown="span">Long-Term Dynamics of Stuart-Landau Model and Kuramoto Model with Non-Local Coupling
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td markdown="span">Adviser
</td>
<td markdown="span">Prof. Gilbert R. Peralta, Dr. rer. nat. 
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td markdown="span">Abstract
</td>
<td markdown="span">Coupled oscillator models have a wide range of applications in pacemaker modeling, deep brain simulation, computer network systems, and supercurrent flows to name a few. We consider the Stuart-Landau system describing coupled oscillatory particles on the plane, and its simplified version, the Kuramoto system, that serves as a prototype model for phase synchronization. A coupling function that depends on the distance of oscillators is applied to the systems. The gradient descents of the Kuramoto systems are constructed to show asymptotic frequency synchronization. In addition, conditions for cluster synchronizations are established analytically. Stable numerical simulations using a predictor-corrector scheme with normally distributed pseudo-random initial conditions are also presented.

<td markdown="span">Title
</td>
<td markdown="span">Analysis and Optimal Control for the Navier--Stokes--Voigt System with Sources in Borel Measures
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td markdown="span">Adviser
</td>
<td markdown="span">Prof. Gilbert R. Peralta, Dr. rer. nat. 
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td markdown="span">Abstract
</td>
<td markdown="span">This dissertation investigates an optimal control problem governed by the Navier--Stokes--Voigt (NSV) equations, which model the motion of incompressible non-Newtonian fluids subject to abrupt external forces. The problem is analyzed within the framework of various measure spaces. The well-posedness of the linearized problem with forcing terms belonging to Bessel potential and Besov spaces is established. We show the existence and uniqueness of very weak solutions of NSV under small-data assumptions by employing the contraction mapping principle. In the two-dimensional case, the Faedo--Galerkin method is applied to the nonlinear component, which, when synthesized with the linear results, yields a global-in-time solution. Notably, this functional framework accommodates both measure and measure-valued sources. Building upon the well-posedness of the state equation, we analyze the associated optimal control problem. We prove the existence of minimizers when the problem is posed in a Banach space with a separable predual. This formulation leads to the consideration of measure controls in space and time, yielding controls that exhibit spatial and temporal sparsity. Finally, optimality conditions are derived and presented for each respective control space. 
</td>
